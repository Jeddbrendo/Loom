Biblical Observational Calendar

This timeline uses highly accurate astronomical approximations to model the ancient Hebrew calendar based on direct observation rather than fixed Rabbinic math.

Epoch & Year Number

Year 1 is calibrated to the creation of Adam in 4026 B.C.E., utilizing a continuous year count.

Spring New Year & Visible Crescent

The year strictly begins in the spring. Months begin on the first visibly observable crescent moon following the dark moon, unburdened by mathematical averages (*molad*).

The Vernal Equinox Rule

Rather than a predetermined 19-year cycle, a 13th leap month (Veadar) is inserted automatically and exclusively to ensure that Passover (Nisan 14) always falls after the Vernal Equinox. No rabbinic postponement rules (*Deḥiyyot*) are applied.

Contents

Welcome to LOOM Timelines

LOOM is a visual timeline tool that lets you place historical events, eras, and stories on an infinite scrollable canvas. You organise events into lanes (rows), group lanes into groups, and navigate freely across thousands of years by panning and zooming.

How LOOM is organised
💡 Tip: You can have multiple projects — each with its own groups, lanes, and events — and switch between them from the top bar.

Navigating the Canvas

The canvas is an infinite scrollable surface. Time runs left to right. You navigate it with pan and zoom.

Pan & Zoom

Toolbar navigation buttons

Reset Fit Mark
  • Reset — returns the view to the default position (year 0, default zoom)
  • Fit — zooms out to show all your events at once F
  • Mark — saves the current view as a named bookmark B
💡 Tip: The timeline ruler at the top shows the current time scale. It automatically adjusts — from billions of years down to individual minutes — as you zoom.

Projects

A project is a self-contained timeline — it has its own groups, lanes, and events. Use different projects for different topics (e.g. "World History", "My Family Tree", "Ancient Rome").

📁+ ✏️ 🗑️
1
Create — click the 📁+ button. Enter a name and press OK. You'll be switched to the new empty project.
2
Switch — use the dropdown selector in the top bar to jump between projects instantly.
3
Rename — click the ✏️ pencil button and type a new name.
4
Delete — click the 🗑️ trash button. This is permanent — all events in that project will be removed.
⚠️ Note: You cannot delete the last remaining project. Export your data first if you want to back it up.

Groups & Lanes

Lanes are the horizontal rows that events sit in. Groups are labelled containers that hold multiple lanes — like folders. Every event must belong to a lane.

Lane header anatomy

Adding groups and lanes

1
Click Group in the toolbar to add a new root group.
2
Click the ⋮ menu on any group and choose Add Lane to add a sub-lane inside it.
3
To reorder lanes, grab the ⠿ drag handle on the left of any header and drag it up or down.
4
Click the ▾ chevron on a group to collapse or expand it, hiding its lanes and events.
💡 Tip: Use the ⋮ menu to rename, change colour, move up/down, or delete any group or lane.

Adding Events

Events are the core of LOOM. There are two types:

Point event vs Span event
1
Click + Event in the toolbar (or press N) to open the event editor.
2
Enter a Title and set the Start Year. Choose BC or AD from the era selector.
3
To make a Span event, check the "Specify End Date" checkbox and fill in the end year.
4
Assign the event to a Lane using the lane dropdown. Every event must belong to a lane.
5
Click Save Event. It will appear on the canvas immediately.

Precision dates

Enable Month & Day in the editor to specify a precise calendar date (day, month, hour, minute). The timeline will display the event with full precision when zoomed in far enough.

Circa dates

Check Circa on either the start or end date to indicate an approximate date. Circa span events render with a fade gradient at the uncertain end, visually communicating the uncertainty.

Editing Events

Single event

1
Click any event on the canvas to open its detail panel on the right.
2
Click the Edit button (pencil icon) in the detail panel to re-open the event editor.
3
Make your changes and click Save Event.

Bulk editor

Click Bulk in the toolbar to open the bulk event editor — a spreadsheet-style table showing all events in the current project. You can edit titles, dates, lanes, and colours for many events at once.

Undo & Redo

Use Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo. LOOM keeps the last 20 actions in history. The undo/redo buttons in the toolbar are greyed out when there is nothing to undo or redo.

💡 Tip: Deleting an event from the detail panel can be undone with Ctrl+Z.

Event Styling

Icon picker

Click the Event Icon button in the editor to open the visual icon picker. Icons are grouped by category. Use the search box to find icons by name.

Colour

Click the colour circle next to the icon picker to open the colour wheel. You can also click any Recent Colour chip or a Theme Colour swatch to apply it instantly.

Lane colour & icon

Lanes and groups can also have their own colour and icon. Click the ⋮ menu on any lane/group header and choose Style.

Rich Text Descriptions

Every event has a Description field with a full visual (WYSIWYG) editor — you format text the same way you would in a word processor, no coding required.

B I U S H2 H3 • List 1. List 🔗 " " — ✕ fmt
The Norman Conquest
The Battle of Hastings in 1066 marked a turning point in English history...
  • William the Conqueror
  • Harold II defeated
  • B I U S — Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough on selected text
  • H2 / H3 — Format the current line as a heading
  • • List / 1. List — Insert a bullet or numbered list
  • 🔗 — Insert a hyperlink (prompts for URL)
  • " " — Blockquote the current line
  • — — Insert a horizontal divider
  • ✕ fmt — Strip all formatting from selected text
💡 Tip: Select text first, then click a toolbar button to apply that format. Standard shortcuts like Ctrl+B for bold also work inside the editor.

Event Library

The Library is a reusable template store for events. Save any event as a template and insert it into any project with one click.

1
Click the Library tab on the right edge of the screen to open the library panel.
2
Use My Library for your own saved templates, Presets for built-in historical events, and Lanes for pre-built lane group templates.
3
Click + New Template at the bottom to create a library entry from scratch.
4
Click any library card and choose Add to Timeline to insert it into the current project.

Collections

Organise your library templates into colour-coded Collections using the left sidebar of the library panel. Click + New at the bottom of the sidebar to create one.

Tags

Library entries support tags for quick filtering. Add tags when creating or editing a template, and click a tag chip at the top of the library to filter by it.

Search

Inline search

Press / or Ctrl+F to focus the search bar in the toolbar. Type to search all events in the current project by title, description, or year. Matching events are highlighted on the canvas.

  • Press Enter or ↓ to jump to the next result
  • Press ↑ to go to the previous result
  • Press Esc to clear the search

Global search

Press Ctrl+G to open the Global Search panel. This searches across all projects at once, letting you find an event even if you can't remember which project it's in. Click a result to navigate directly to it.

Bookmarks

Bookmarks save a named snapshot of the current canvas position and zoom level. Useful for jumping quickly between very different time periods in the same project.

1
Navigate to a view you want to save, then click Mark in the toolbar (or press B).
2
Enter a name for the bookmark and press OK.
3
The bookmark appears as a chip at the bottom of the canvas. Click it to jump back to that view instantly.
4
Hover a bookmark chip and click the × that appears to delete it.

Import & Export

📊 CSV ⬆️ Export JSON 🖼️ PNG 🔗 Share HTML ⬇️ Import JSON

Export JSON

Saves the entire current project (all lanes and events) as a .json file. Use this to back up your work or move it to another device. Re-import it with the Import JSON button.

CSV Import

Import events from a spreadsheet. Your CSV needs at minimum a Title column and a Start Year column. Optional columns include: End Year, Lane, Description, Color, Icon, Image, Source.

💡 Example CSV:
Title, Start Year, End Year, Lane
Battle of Hastings, 1066,, England
Roman Empire, -27, 476, Rome

PNG Export

Renders the entire timeline (all events, all lanes) to a high-resolution PNG image. Useful for presentations or printing.

Shareable HTML

Exports a fully self-contained .html file with your timeline rendered as a read-only interactive page — no LOOM account needed to view it. Send it to anyone.
